The Golden Rules of Authentic Foundry Debossing
The 'O' Rule Must form a mathematically perfect geometric circle, never an elliptical zero.
The 'L' Foot The horizontal stroke is strikingly short relative to its vertical stem.
The 'V' Apex The bottom vertex finishes in a needle-sharp point, not a rounded basin.
