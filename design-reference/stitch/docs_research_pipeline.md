## 2. Source Credibility Taxonomy

Every ingested web reference receives an explicit provenance weighting score:

| Source Category | Credibility Weight | Licensing & Usage Rule | Example Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Official Brand Information** | 0.95 | Fair use citations only; quote verbatim specs; never claim trademark endorsement. | LVMH Investor Relations, Official Louis Vuitton Care Guides, Registered Patents. |
| **Tier 2: Authorized Provider Docs** | 0.90 | Citable under enterprise SLA agreements. | Entrupy API Documentation, Published Optical Whitepapers. |
| **Tier 3: Independent Forensic Experts** | 0.85 | Academic & expert citations with mandatory attribution and direct permalinks. | Heritage Auctions Catalogues, The Purse Blog Historical Guides, Vintage Fashion Guild. |
| **Tier 4: Community & Forums** | 0.40 | Demarcated as *Unverified Empirical Discussion*; never promotes to AI baseline without physical specimen lab verification. | PurseForum threads, Reddit r/LegitCheck, TikTok reseller claims. |

## 3. Conflicting Evidence Resolution Engine

When a newly extracted claim contradicts an existing knowledge record:
1. **Automated Diff Generation**: Flags conflicting variables (e.g., *Source A claims 6 stitches; Atelier Rule #RL-LV-014 enforces 5 stitches*).
2. **Conflict Severity Rating**:
   - `HIGH`: Direct contradiction in hardware alloy, font kerning, or stitch count.
   - `MEDIUM`: Variation attributed to production transition years (e.g., date code to RFID transition between Nov 2020 and March 2021).
   - `LOW`: Semantic nuance in leather grain terminology.
3. **Escalation to Peer Review Quorum**: High-severity conflicts freeze promotion into the automated AI matching vector pool until 2 master appraisers resolve the discrepancy.
