## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-tier digital sanctum tailored for ultra-luxury goods authentication, provenance tracking, and forensic verification. Designed for luxury houses, elite appraisers, and discerning collectors, the visual tone balances the clinical precision of forensic intelligence with the timeless, sovereign prestige of high fashion and bespoke watchmaking.

The interface adheres to an **Editorial Luxury Glassmorphic** aesthetic:
- **Atmospheric Nocturne:** Canvas layers built from deep onyx and volcanic charcoal create an immersive, low-glare dark sanctuary that highlights high-resolution product forensics.
- **Precious Accents:** Champagne gold and spun bullion highlights are applied strictly to verify status, signal primary CTAs, and mark cryptographic seals.
- **Architectural Restraint:** Lavish typography and sharp geometric structural lines evoke bespoke print editorial monographs, rejecting loud SaaS tropes in favor of hushed, dignified authority.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast chiaroscuro between deep midnight surfaces and warm champagne luminescence.

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`) & Secondary (`#E5C365`):** Reserved for authentication marks, verified ledger seals, active states, key data metrics, and pivotal focal triggers.
- **Champagne Highlight (`#F3E5AB`):** Applied for specular gleams, badge counter-edges, and high-priority micro-indicators.
- **Typography & Content:**
  - **Silk Cream (`#FAF7F2`):** Primary editorial headlines, serial digits, and key readouts.
  - **Warm Ivory (`#E8E2D8`):** Running body prose, descriptive metadata, and subheaders.
  - **Muted Oyster (`#9C968B`):** Secondary metadata, inactive states, and structural timestamps.
- **Surfaces & Containers:**
  - **Base Canvas (`#0A0A0C`):** Root application canvas.
  - **Charcoal Sanctum (`#121216`):** Base container, drawer, and persistent nav surface.
  - **Raised Glass (`#1A1920` at 65% opacity):** Frosted forensic inspection cards and overlay panels.
- **Structural Trims:**
  - **Muted Bronze Border (`#2A261D`):** Passive hairline grids, card perimeters, and split rules.
  - **Lit Bronze Accent (`#3E3626`):** Focused borders, hover frames, and elevated card dividers.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses the marriage between haute horlogerie heritage and microscopic forensic analysis.

- **Bodoni Moda:** High-contrast, razor-sharp serifs bring haute couture prestige to display titles, item attributions, and certificate headlines. It must never be set in all-caps for long strings.
- **Hanken Grotesk:** Clean, contemporary grotesque sans-serif that ensures absolute optical clarity for micro-serial numbers, spectroscopic readouts, ledger hashes, and structural UI labels.
- **`label-caps` Rule:** All category taxonomy, status labels, verification phases, and table headers must be set in uppercase with tracking expanded to `+0.14em` to mirror physical luxury registry engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an architectural gallery, utilizing expansive negative space to allow rare physical and digital assets room to breathe.

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`>1024px`) shifting to a 6-column grid on tablet (`768px-1023px`), and a single-column stacked system on mobile (`<767px`).
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous outer margins (`3rem` on desktop) frame the canvas like a matted art frame. Section divides are punctuated by hair-thin bronze rules (`#2A261D`) rather than heavy physical blocks.
- **Inspection Split:** Diagnostic views use asymmetrical splits (7 columns for high-magnification multi-angle imagery, 5 columns for cryptographic ledger and material authenticity analysis).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, generic drop shadows in favor of tinted gold luminescence, deep backdrop filtration, and metallic edge articulation.

- **Surface 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#0A0A0C`. Background for full-bleed viewport.
- **Surface 1 (Structural Shelves):** Solid `#121216` with a `1px` solid border of `#2A261D`. Used for navigation matrices, toolbars, and inspection footers.
- **Surface 2 (Floating Forensic Card):** Frosted glass composition: `background: rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.72); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px);`. Encased in a composite border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)` fading to `#2A261D`).
- **Surface 3 (Inspection Modals & Flyouts):** `background: rgba(22, 22, 28, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(24px);`. Enriched with an ambient gold under-glow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 50px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 40px -10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08);`.
- **Specular Border Accent:** On interactive focus or authenticated asset highlights, card borders transition from muted bronze (`#2A261D`) to a dynamic radial gold metallic sheen (`#E5C365`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework is tailored and architectural, avoiding aggressive roundedness to preserve an air of formal precision and bespoke luxury craft.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for tactile buttons, cryptographic hash badges, input inputs, and tag pills.
- **Container Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for diagnostic cards, modal sheets, and image inspection frames.
- **Micro Radii:** Precision tools, zoom locators, and tabular chips honor sharp, restrained corners rather than playful circular blobs.
- **Luxury Seal Insets:** Verified status emblems and watermark medallions use chamfered or subtle double-bordered framing to evoke certified hallmarks on fine jewelry.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Seals
- **Primary Authentication Button:** Solid champagne gold background (`#D4AF37`) with ink text (`#0A0A0C`), set in `label-caps`. Hover activates an inner gold gleam (`#F3E5AB`) and subtle ambient gold drop glow (`0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background encased in a `1px` bronze border (`#3E3626`), typography in silk cream (`#FAF7F2`). Hover shifts border to `#D4AF37` with silk cream copy.
- **Destructive Action:** Deep crimson-tinted charcoal (`#1F1113`) bound by an oxidized ruby border (`#5C242A`), text rendered in muted crimson ivory.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background set to semi-translucent charcoal (`rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.6)`), styled with a `1px` border of `#2A261D` and `rounded-sm`. 
- Placeholder text in muted oyster (`#9C968B`). 
- On focus, border transitions smoothly to champagne gold (`#D4AF37`) with a delicate internal gold wash (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.03)`).

### Verification Badges & Chips
- **Authenticated Seal:** Matte black surface with a `1px` champagne metallic border (`#D4AF37`), gold text (`#E5C365`), preceded by an illuminated gold micro-pip (`#F3E5AB`).
- **Analysis In-Progress:** Subtle bronze fill (`#1E1A14`) paired with an amber indicator and oyster typography.
- **Flagged / Counterfeit Warning:** Charcoal surface edged in oxidized copper-red (`#7E2D2D`), accompanied by silk cream alert typography.

### Forensic Cards & Ledger Tables
- Cards feature frosted glass layering (`rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.72)`) bordered with hairline bronze (`#2A261D`).
- Inspection lists and ledger logs use hairline horizontal separators (`rgba(42, 38, 29, 0.6)`). Hovering over a ledger row creates a subtle champagne-tinted illumination sweep across the row background (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.02)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom square markers for checkboxes (`16px × 16px`) with razor corners (`rounded: 2px`). Inactive state is a bronze rim (`#3E3626`) on charcoal (`#0A0A0C`). 
- Checked state features a champagne gold border (`#D4AF37`) and an inner gold emblem fill.
